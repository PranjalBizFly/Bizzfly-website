# Cookie Privacy and Compliance

## Objective
Handle personal data, cookies and consent responsibly.

## Policies
Where applicable provide:
- Privacy Policy
- Terms
- Cookie Policy
- Refund Policy
- Cancellation Policy
- Security Policy
- Disclaimer

## Data Collection
Document:
- What data is collected
- Why it is collected
- Where it is stored
- How long it is retained
- Who receives it

## Consent
Where legally required support:
- Cookie consent
- Analytics consent
- Marketing consent
- Preference management
- Consent records

## User Rights
Where applicable support:
- Access
- Correction
- Deletion
- Export
- Consent withdrawal

## Rules
Do not invent legal compliance claims. Do not claim certifications the business does not have. Legal documents should be reviewed for the actual business and applicable jurisdiction.
