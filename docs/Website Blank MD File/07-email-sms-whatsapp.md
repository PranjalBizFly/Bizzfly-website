# Email SMS and WhatsApp Communication

## Objective
Standardize transactional and business communications.

## Email Types
Support where required:
- Contact notification
- Auto reply
- Welcome
- Email verification
- Password reset
- OTP
- Payment confirmation
- Invoice
- Refund
- Appointment confirmation
- Reminder
- Admin alert

## SMS and WhatsApp
Use only where required:
- OTP
- Transactional notification
- Appointment reminder
- Lead follow up
- Payment status

## Requirements
Use a reliable provider, secure credentials and environment variables. Handle provider failures gracefully.

## Templates
Templates should include:
- Clear subject
- Brand identity
- Relevant data
- Contact details
- Required legal footer
- Unsubscribe mechanism for marketing messages

## Rules
Do not send marketing communication without appropriate consent. Do not expose personal data in URLs or logs.
